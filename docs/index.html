Moving to Aster

Aster is a nonprofit that hosts accounts on the open web. Moving there changes where your account lives — you keep the same account ID, so you're not starting over.

Your handle may change to an Aster domain. You can do this at any point, and at this workshop someone can walk you through it — come to the Start here station.
